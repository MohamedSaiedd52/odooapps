Letter of Credit Management (LC)

Manage documentary letters of credit from opening to execution — with automatic journal entries.

إدارة الاعتمادات المستندية من الفتح حتى التنفيذ — بقيود محاسبية تلقائية وواجهة عربية بالكامل.

What does it do?

A dedicated Letter of Credit (LC) register inside Odoo Accounting. Each LC document holds the bank journal, LC number, date, amount, currency and bank charges — then follows a clear Draft → Running → Done workflow. At every stage the module posts the matching journal entry for you, so the LC account, the bank and the charges are always in sync, in the original LC currency.

Workflow

STEP 1

Draft — مسودة

Register the LC: bank journal & account, LC number, date, amount, currency, bank expenses, LC settlement account and expense account. A sequence gives every document its own reference (LOC/00001).

STEP 2

Running — جاري

One click posts the opening entry: the LC amount moves from the bank to the LC settlement account and the bank charges are booked as an expense — all in the LC currency.

STEP 3

Done — منتهي

When the credit is executed, the closing entry clears the LC settlement account against the bank. Both entries stay linked to the document through smart buttons.

✔ A Cancel action is available while the document is still a draft.

Key Features

✔ Full LC register under Accounting → Journal Entries ✔ Automatic opening & closing journal entries
✔ Multi-currency: amounts posted in the LC currency ✔ Dedicated field for bank charges / LC expenses
✔ Smart buttons to open the running & executed entries ✔ Automatic sequence LOC/00001 per document
✔ Access group: only authorized users see the LC menu ✔ Arabic user interface — واجهة عربية جاهزة

Document Fields

Field Purpose
Bank Journal / Bank Account — دفتر حساب البنك The bank paying for the LC (account taken from the journal automatically)
LC Number & Date — رقم وتاريخ الإعتماد The official credit reference used on every journal item
LC Amount & Currency — مبلغ وعملة الإعتماد Value of the credit, posted as amount-currency on all entries
Bank Expenses — مصروفات الإعتماد البنكية LC fees booked to the expense account in the opening entry
LC Settlement Account — الحساب المقابل Current-asset account holding the LC amount while it is running
Expense Account — حساب المصروفات Where the bank charges are recorded

Compatibility

Works on Odoo 17, 18 and 19 (Community and Enterprise). Depends only on base and account — no extra libraries needed.
