Custom Ledger Currency Total

Real multi-currency totals in the General Ledger, the Partner Ledger and Journal Items

Odoo 17.0 Enterprise Accounting Reports No configuration needed

What does this module do?

In standard Odoo, the "Amount in Currency" column of the ledger reports stays empty on account and partner lines unless the account is locked to one specific foreign currency — so when your invoices, credit notes and vendor bills are booked in EUR, GBP or any other mix of currencies, you never see a total.

This module computes and injects those totals automatically, respecting all the report filters (date range, journals, posted-entries-only…):

📊 General Ledger
Every account line and the report Total line show the sum of Amount in Currency of their journal items.		 🤝 Partner Ledger
Every partner line and the Total row show the net Amount Currency across all of that partner's documents.		 📋 Journal Items
The list view gets a total aggregate on the Amount in Currency column.

Case 1 — General Ledger: totals per account

The receivable account contains documents in two different currencies (EUR invoices & credit note, and a GBP invoice). The module totals them on the account line: 1,200 + 2,300 − 500 (EUR) + 4,000 (GBP) = 7,000.00 — and does the same for payables, sales and expense accounts.

Case 2 — Partner Ledger: net total per partner & grand total

Acme Corporation has two EUR invoices and one EUR credit note → the partner line shows the net 3,000.00. The GBP customer (4,000.00) and the EUR vendor bill (−1,500.00) are totalled the same way, and the report's Total row shows the grand total 5,500.00.

Case 3 — The multi-currency journal items behind the reports

The demo data used in the screenshots above: three EUR documents and one GBP invoice posted on the same receivable account. The module also adds a column total on this Journal Items list (displayed when the filtered lines share a single currency).

Good to know

  • Totals are computed on the fly from the journal items matching the current report filters (period, journals, posted entries only…).
  • Works with any number of currencies — EUR, GBP, USD or your own.
  • Requires the Multi-Currencies access right (standard Odoo hides the currency column without it).
  • Zero configuration: install it and open your ledgers.
