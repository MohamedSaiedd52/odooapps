Salla Connector

Connect your Salla store to Odoo — products, orders and customers, synced automatically.

اربط متجرك في منصة سلة مع أودو — مزامنة المنتجات والطلبات والعملاء تلقائياً.

Connect in one click

Paste your Salla app Client ID and Secret, hit Connect to Salla and approve — that’s it. The module handles the OAuth2 flow and refreshes expired tokens automatically, and a Test Connection button tells you instantly that everything works.

Products, imported clean

Pull your whole Salla catalog with one action: name, price, SKU, sales description and the public store URL. Existing products are matched by Salla ID or SKU — no duplicates — and the raw Salla payload is kept on the product for full traceability.

Orders flow straight into Sales

Every Salla order becomes a normal Odoo quotation with its customer (deduplicated by mobile/email), its lines and prices. Products missing from Odoo are created on the fly so an order never fails to import. The Salla ID and live status sit on a dedicated tab.

Key Features

✔ OAuth2 connect with automatic token refresh ✔ One-click product import with pagination
✔ Order import with customers & lines ✔ Duplicate-safe matching by Salla ID, SKU, mobile & email
✔ Live webhooks for product & order updates ✔ Webhook HMAC signature verification
✔ Salla tab on products & orders with raw payload ✔ Covered by automated tests

Compatibility

Works on Odoo 17, 18 and 19 (Community and Enterprise). Depends on sale_management, stock and account.
