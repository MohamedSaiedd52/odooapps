Hijri Date Everywhere

Hijri (Islamic) dates beside every Gregorian date — on invoices, payments and quotations, on their PDF reports, and in a live two-way date converter. Works on Odoo 17, 18 and 19, Community & Enterprise. No external libraries, no JavaScript.

On the documents

Invoice, payment and sale order forms show the Hijri date right beside the Gregorian one — computed automatically, always in sync.

On the PDFs too

Invoice and quotation PDF reports print the Hijri date under each Gregorian date — ready for customers and authorities.

Moon-sighting aware

A per-company adjustment of ±3 days aligns the computed date with local moon sighting or the Umm al-Qura calendar.

Pick your format, set your adjustment

Latin transliteration, Arabic script or numeric — per company, with a live “today” preview while you configure.

Hijri date on every invoice

The invoice form shows the Hijri date under the invoice date. Payments and quotations get the same treatment.

Printed on the PDF reports

Invoice date and due date both carry their Hijri equivalent on the printed document.

Live two-way converter

Convert as you type: Gregorian to Hijri in three formats at once, and Hijri to Gregorian with the weekday. Impossible days get a friendly note instead of an error.

Sales orders included

The order date in Hijri, converted in the user’s timezone.

For developers: one-line extension point

Need a Hijri date on any other model? Inherit the bundled mixin and add a computed Char field that calls self._hijri_format(record.date). The tabular conversion engine (Julian Day Numbers, 30-year leap cycle) is pure Python with zero dependencies and is round-trip safe across the ±3-day adjustment.

What you get

  • Hijri dates on invoice, payment and sale order forms and their PDF reports
  • Live two-way Gregorian ↔ Hijri converter with weekday and validation
  • Three display formats: Latin, Arabic script, numeric — per company
  • Moon-sighting / Umm al-Qura adjustment (±3 days), round-trip safe
  • Timezone-aware conversion for datetime fields
  • Developer mixin to add Hijri dates to any model in one line
  • No JavaScript, no external Python packages — installs anywhere
