MS Salesperson Report & Filter

See who really sold it — track the salesperson on the invoice, not only on the sales order.

Odoo 17 Odoo 18 Odoo 19 LGPL-3 Community & Enterprise

Why this module?

The standard Sales Analysis report always shows the salesperson taken from the sales order (user_id). In many companies, however, the salesperson recorded on the customer invoice (invoice_user_id) is a different person — invoices get reassigned, commissions are paid on invoiced (not ordered) revenue, or the accounting team books the invoice under another user. This module lets managers report and filter on the invoice salesperson and compare both views side by side.

  • Sales Analysis report: a new field “Salesperson (Invoice)” — taken from the first posted customer invoice linked to the sales order — available as a pivot row and a Group By option.
  • Journal Items (Accounting): a new filter “My Sales (by Invoice)” — instantly shows the journal entries of the invoices you are responsible for.

1 · Invoice Salesperson in the Sales Analysis report

Open Sales → Reporting → Sales and switch to the pivot view. Every period now breaks down by the salesperson on the posted customer invoice. Orders that are not invoiced yet appear under None, so you can also spot uninvoiced revenue at a glance.

The same field is available in the search panel as a dedicated Group By option, right below the standard Salesperson — so you can compare “who took the order” vs “who invoiced it” in two clicks.

2 · “My Sales (by Invoice)” filter in Journal Items

In Accounting → Journal Items the module adds a one-click filter that keeps only the journal entries of customer invoices and credit notes where you are the invoice salesperson (move_id.invoice_user_id = current user).

One click and the list shrinks to your own invoice lines — perfect for reviewing your receivables, checking commissions, or reconciling your customers.

How it works (technical)

  • Adds invoice_user_id (Many2one → res.users) to the sale.report SQL model via the standard _select_additional_fields() hook — no core model is modified.
  • The value is resolved by a correlated subquery: the first posted customer invoice / credit note (earliest invoice date) whose origin matches the sale order, in the same company.
  • The Journal Items filter is a plain search-view inheritance on account.view_account_move_line_filter — zero performance impact.
  • No new tables, no cron jobs, no JavaScript — safe to install and uninstall at any time.

Compatibility

Tested on Odoo 17, 18 and 19 (Community and Enterprise). Depends only on the standard sale and account modules.
