HR Loan Accounting

Complete employee loan management — loan requests with installment schedules, payroll deduction, automatic journal entries on approval, a Journal Entries smart button, and an installment delay wizard.

Odoo 18 Human Resources Accounting

Key Features

📑 Automatic Journal Entry

When a loan is approved, a balanced journal entry is created and posted automatically: debit the Loan Account (employee receivable), credit the Treasury Account (cash/bank disbursement).

📚 Journal Entries Smart Button

A smart button on the loan form shows the number of linked journal entries (matched by the loan reference) and opens them in one click.

⏰ Delay Installments Wizard

Postpone the repayment schedule of an approved loan: shift every unpaid installment forward by N months. Paid installments are never touched.
Validation guard: a loan cannot be approved until the Journal, Loan Account and Treasury Account are set — so no disbursement ever goes unrecorded.

How It Works

1. Set the accounting details on the loan request

Three new fields are added to the loan form: Journal (bank/cash), Loan Account (debit side) and Treasury Account (credit side). Compute the installment schedule, submit, and approve.

2. Approval posts the disbursement entry

On approval the journal entry is created and posted automatically. The Journal Entries smart button appears in the button box, and the Delay Installments button becomes available in the header.

3. The posted journal entry

The entry debits the Loan Account and credits the Treasury Account for the full loan amount, with the loan number as reference.

4. Delay the repayment schedule

Open the Delay Installments wizard on an approved loan, choose the number of months, and submit. Only unpaid installments are rescheduled.

5. Updated installment schedule

After the delay, unpaid installment dates are shifted forward while paid lines and running totals (Total / Paid / Balance) stay consistent.

6. Loan requests overview

All loan requests with their lifecycle states: Draft → Submitted → Approved / Refused / Canceled.

Accounting Logic

Account Debit Credit
Loan Account (employee loan receivable) Loan Amount —
Treasury Account (bank / cash) — Loan Amount

The entry is posted in the selected bank/cash journal with the loan number (e.g. LO/0001) as its reference.

Technical Details

  • Dependencies: hr, hr_payroll, account.
  • Models: hr.loan, hr.loan.line — loan requests and installments, with journal_id, loan_account_id, treasury_account_id, move_count.
  • New wizard: hr.loan.delay.wizard — shifts unpaid installment dates by N months.
  • Approval flow: action_approve() — validates the accounting setup, then creates and posts the disbursement entry.
  • Payroll: installments due in the payslip period are added as LO other inputs and deducted by the bundled salary rule.
  • Unit tests included: approval guard, posted-entry correctness, delay wizard behaviour (tests/test_hr_loan_accounting.py).
