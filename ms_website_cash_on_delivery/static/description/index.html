Website Cash on Delivery (COD)

Let your eCommerce customers pay in cash when the courier arrives — with COD fees, amount limits and zip-code rules, all configurable.

COD at the checkout

Cash on Delivery appears next to your other payment methods on the website payment step. The customer picks it, confirms the order, and gets your delivery instructions — the order is confirmed in Odoo exactly like any other online order.

Simple, powerful configuration

One payment provider record controls everything: a fixed COD fee and/or a percentage fee, a minimum order amount, and an optional list of allowed zip codes. Odoo’s native maximum amount, country and currency restrictions work on top of it.

COD orders, clearly marked

Every COD order is flagged automatically and the configured fee is appended as a separate order line — so invoices, taxes and reports stay correct. A dedicated Cash on Delivery filter in the orders list shows all pending collections at a glance.

Collection sheet for the driver

Select the day’s COD orders and print one COD Collection Sheet: customer, phone, shipping address, amount to collect and a signature column — everything the courier needs on a single page.

Key Features

✔ Cash on Delivery option on the website checkout ✔ Fixed + percentage COD fee, added as an order line
✔ Minimum order amount rule (native maximum on top) ✔ Restrict by zip codes, countries & currencies
✔ Orders flagged & filterable as Cash on Delivery ✔ Printable COD Collection Sheet for the courier
✔ Customizable confirmation message for the customer ✔ No external services — 100% inside Odoo

Compatibility

Works on Odoo 17, 18 and 19 (Community and Enterprise). Depends on website_sale and payment_custom only. Covered by automated tests.
