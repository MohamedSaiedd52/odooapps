MS Partner Ledger – Invoice Details Drill-down

Unfold any invoice right inside the Partner Ledger and see what was sold — product, quantity and line total — without leaving the report.

Odoo 17 Odoo 18 Odoo 19 Enterprise (account_reports)

Why this module?

The Partner Ledger answers “how much does this customer owe?” — but the moment a customer calls about an invoice, the very next question is “what was this invoice for?”. In standard Odoo you have to leave the report, open the invoice, look at the lines, and come back.

With this module every invoice and credit note in the Partner Ledger becomes unfoldable: one click on its arrow expands the product lines — Product — Quantity (UoM) | Line Total — right inside the report, on screen and in the PDF / XLSX exports.

1 · An unfold arrow on every invoice line

Open Accounting → Reporting → Partner Ledger and expand a partner. Every line that belongs to a real invoice or credit note now carries its own arrow. Manual journal entries (MISC) are left untouched — they have no product lines to show.

2 · Product lines with quantity and amount

Click the arrow and the invoice expands into its product lines. Each row shows the product, the quantity with its unit of measure, and the line subtotal placed in the Debit column for invoices (or the Credit column for refunds) — so the details always mirror, and add up to, the invoice line above them.

How it works (technical)

  • Inherits the Enterprise report handler account.partner.ledger.report.handler — after the standard partner expansion, every move line that belongs to an invoice (out_invoice / out_refund / in_invoice / in_refund) is tagged unfoldable with a custom expand function.
  • Child rows are built with the official account.report._get_generic_line_id() helper — no hand-crafted line IDs, so _parse_line_id never breaks.
  • PDF & XLSX ready: in export mode the invoice details are expanded automatically, so the printed report shows the same drill-down as the screen.
  • No change to the report definition, no new columns, no stored fields, no JavaScript — safe to install and uninstall at any time.

Compatibility

Tested on Odoo 17, 18 and 19 Enterprise. Requires the Enterprise reporting engine (account_reports). Works with all standard Partner Ledger filters (dates, partners, posted/draft entries, multi-company).
